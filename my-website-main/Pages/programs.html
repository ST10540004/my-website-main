Programs

Programs designed for transformation.

From schools to campuses, communities, and career preparation — every program is built to equip young people with the tools they need to thrive.

01

Adopt-a-School Program

Supporting underperforming schools through tutoring, motivational talks, career guidance, life-skills education, and post-matric opportunities.

02

Campus Youth Quake

Equipping students beyond the classroom through leadership formation, academic excellence, service culture, and community impact.

03

Community Outreach

Promoting moral values and social awareness through workshops, sports, campaigns, clean-up drives, and rehabilitation support.

04

Young Professionals

Preparing final-year students and professionals with ethics, financial literacy, emotional intelligence, and career mapping.

05

Youth Quake Tour

A rural career exposure tour helping learners access bursary guidance, applications, and purpose after high school.

06

Lean-On-Me Mentorship

Mentoring teenagers through discipline, academic excellence, leadership, personal vision, and long-term growth.
