About

Built to shape futures through youth mobilisation, education, and mentoring.

Youth Quake was established in 1998, born from the need to address the socio-economic issues facing young people. We bring together volunteers, students, and young professionals to influence schools, communities, and future leaders.

Our Story

  • Promote moral values in schools, campuses, and communities
  • Equip youth in all fields of life
  • Mobilise and unite youth for meaningful national impact
