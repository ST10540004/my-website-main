About Us

Built to shape futures through youth mobilisation, education, and mentoring.

Youth Quake was established in 1998, born from the need to address the socio-economic issues facing young people. We bring together volunteers, students, and young professionals to influence schools, communities, and future leaders across South Africa.

Our Story & Pillars

  • Promote moral values and leadership in schools, campuses, and communities
  • Equip youth through skills development across all professional fields
  • Mobilise and unite young leaders for meaningful national impact
